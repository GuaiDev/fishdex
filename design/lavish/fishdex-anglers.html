How it scales

One shared core, three surfaces — chosen by how you fish, switchable any time.

Explorer = the default home

Little data, little admin. The carousel is where everyone starts; it stays beautiful when a person has caught three fish or thirty.

Lifelister = density on demand

Once the list grows, a one-at-a-time view gets slow. The list gives totals, search, sort and every species in one scroll.

Specialist = depth on one fish

If 90% of your catches are one species, the curated view is that species: records, trend, waters, history.

Same spine throughout

Photos, water biomes and the auto-chosen stat are shared. Only the layout changes, so it feels like one guide, not three apps.

Which is the right cut?

Native controls register directly; pick one to queue it, or annotate the phone screens and comment.
