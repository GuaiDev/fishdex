The idea

Four quiet decisions that keep it a field guide, not a game.

One fish at a time

The species fills the frame, with the next ones waiting half-seen at the edges. Swipe to move through them; nothing competes for attention.

Its own water

Behind every fish is the water it lives in. For now one treatment (a soft underwater column, tinted by habitat); we can branch into distinct biomes later.

One honest number

No point values. The card shows the single most relevant fact, chosen for you: your biggest of that fish, or how many you've logged, or nothing but a silhouette.

Undiscovered stay hidden

Fish you haven't caught appear only as a silhouette in the water, so the guide grows with your season instead of spoiling the list.

What changed

From the card-game take to the field guide.

Before (too gamey)

  • Rarity gems and "Holo" labels
  • Dex numbers and set symbols
  • Cards in plastic pockets, foils, stickers
  • Read as a collectible toy, not a fishing tool

After (field guide)

  • Real photography of the actual fish
  • Each species in its home water
  • Swipe carousel, one species centred
  • One earned stat, no points or rarities
