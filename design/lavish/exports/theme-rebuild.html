Deep Current

A hushed, high-end tackle catalogue. Warm near-black paper, brushed gold, an optical serif for the collection title, monospaced numbers for the catch data. Quiet and premium — reads as expensive without shouting.

Bright, cool, and clinical. Off-white paper, deep teal ink, a coral accent for new finds, an expressive grotesque for headings. Reads like a well-made field guide or a modern museum label.
