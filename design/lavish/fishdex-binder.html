What changed

From the flat pass to the binder.

Before (flat / corporate)

  • Rows of plain list cells, no collectible feel
  • One accent, everything neutral and equal
  • Progress read as a number, not a goal
  • No reason to want the next catch

After (collector's binder)

  • A real 9-pocket page you flip through
  • Species as type-colored cards with rarity
  • Holo foil on the cards worth showing off
  • Master-set ring and "still need" slots to pull you in
